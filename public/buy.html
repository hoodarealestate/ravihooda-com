Buyer's Guide

What You Need to Know Before You Buy

1

Get Pre-Approved First

A mortgage pre-approval tells you your real budget and shows sellers you're a serious buyer. We can refer you to trusted lenders who move fast.

2

Understand Your Deposit

Typically 5% of the purchase price, due within 24 hours of an accepted offer. It's held in trust and applied to your down payment at closing — not an extra cost.

3

Know Your Offer Conditions

Financing and home inspection conditions protect you. For condos, add a status certificate review condition — it reveals the building's finances and any special assessments.

4

Budget for Closing Costs

Land transfer tax (double in Toronto — provincial plus municipal), legal fees, title insurance and adjustments typically add 1.5%–4% of the purchase price beyond your down payment.

5

Buyer Representation Is Free to You

In most transactions, the seller pays both sides' commission. Having your own agent means someone negotiating only for your interests, at no direct cost to you.

6

Time Your Purchase

New listings are down 14.1% year-over-year — well-priced homes are moving fast even in a buyer-friendlier market. Being pre-approved and ready lets you move the moment the right one appears.
