Seller's Guide

Preparing Your Property for Sale

1

Declutter & Depersonalize

Pack away excess furniture, family photos and personal items. Buyers need to picture themselves living there — clear countertops and closets go a long way.

2

Handle Small Repairs First

Leaky faucets, chipped paint, squeaky doors, loose handles — small flaws buyers notice and use to negotiate. Fix them before you list, not after an offer.

3

Deep Clean Everything

Carpets, windows, grout, baseboards. A spotless home photographs better and consistently shows better in person — it's the highest-return prep step there is.

4

Boost Curb Appeal

Trim the lawn, clear the walkway, freshen up the front door, update house numbers. First impressions happen before a buyer steps inside.

5

Professional Photos & Staging

We arrange professional photography and a staging consultation as part of our listing service — this is where your home's first online impression is made.

6

Gather Your Documents Early

Survey or RPR, most recent property tax statement, mortgage payout figure, condo status certificate (if applicable), and permits/warranties for any past renovations.

7

Price It Right From Day One

Overpricing costs more than underpricing — homes that sit get stigmatized and eventually sell for less. We base your price on real, current comparables, not guesswork.

8

Know the Timeline

List → showings → offers → conditions (financing, inspection) → firm sale → closing. In the current market, expect roughly 30–60 days from list to a firm deal.
